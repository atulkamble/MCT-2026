# Six training deliveries: MTM route

The portal requires **six legitimate, eligible training deliveries** logged against the MCT email for the 2026–2027 cohort. **Custom courses** may qualify, subject to Microsoft's rules.

## Record a real class

1. Register at https://www.metricsthatmatter.com/NewMCTInMTM/EnrollMCT.aspx if necessary.
2. Activate your registration email token within 24 hours; use Forgot Password if it expires.
3. Log into https://www.metricsthatmatter.com.
4. Select **Data Collection → Classes → Create Class**.
5. Find a course, choose learning method and client, enter start date and create the class.
6. At the actual class conclusion, share its class-specific learner evaluation link.
7. Ask learners to submit authentic feedback.
8. Review the class record and performance; export reports for your records.
9. Allow **up to 14 days** for the MCT portal to reflect new logs.
10. Confirm **MCT Portal → My Profile** shows sufficient eligible classes.

## Can I repeat/reattempt this route?

**Logging six classes isn't a timed exam.** The supplied guidance does not state a finite number of times you may record additional **genuine** training deliveries. If you lack six accepted classes, check why records are missing or ineligible, then continue delivering and logging additional eligible classes **within the applicable renewal period**. There is no guarantee each entry will count; Microsoft determines eligibility.

**Important:** After selecting the **MCT Renewal Assessment** you **cannot revert** to this six-class route.

## Session duration

The supplied September 2026 MTM guide does not define a minimum eligible class duration. Microsoft's Community Hub references six sessions or six days per calendar year; check the current cohort rules and seek a formal answer from **mctprog@microsoft.com** before relying on short sessions.

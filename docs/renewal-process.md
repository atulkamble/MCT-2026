# Renewal decision and submission process

The supplied portal announcement applies to MCT statuses expiring **1 October 2026 through 31 May 2027**, with applications available up to 90 days prior to expiration.

## Route A: Six teaching deliveries in MTM

Deliver **six genuine eligible sessions/classes** and record them in Metrics That Matter against the **email address used to sign into the MCT Portal**. The portal says custom courses may count. MTM syncs biweekly (up to 14 days). View eligible counts using **MCT Portal → My Profile**. Training delivery is not a test; the guide does not specify a limited number of MTM recording attempts. If a class is not recognized, investigate its eligibility and logging; further genuine eligible classes may be logged while renewal remains open. Microsoft makes the eligibility decision.

Microsoft's renewal policy describes **six training deliveries within the renewal period**, and a Microsoft Community update discusses **six sessions or six days per calendar year**. Confirm exactly which measurement applies to your cohort in the official portal. The source materials provided do **not** define a minimum eligible class length.

## Route B: MCT Renewal Assessment

- Complete the **entire renewal application** through the [MCT Portal](https://aka.ms/mctportal).
- Follow the enrollment prompt for the **assessment**. **Warning: selecting the assessment means you cannot revert to Route A.**
- Register for **Yoodli** from the link emailed to your MCT-linked address.
- Go to **Yoodli → My Learning → MCT Renewal Assessment**, *not* generic Roleplays.
- Complete **10 modules in order** (estimated **45–90 minutes** total).
- Pass the **Program Agreement Quiz** (≥70%, one counted attempt).
- Pass the **AI-moderated teach-back** (≥70%; best of the first two counted attempts).
- Confirm at least one **active Microsoft certification**.
- Pause **between modules** only; start a scored activity when ready to finish.
- The guide says **no retakes of the complete assessment**; if status lapses, a fee-based MCT Instructional Skills Assessment and possibly a three-day onboarding experience may be needed.

## Completion

If you pass, allow **up to 72 business hours** for the result to appear in the portal, then pay the annual MCT Program fee. If the Yoodli email doesn't arrive or the MCT-linked mailbox is inaccessible, contact **mctprog@microsoft.com**. Portal problems: **mctpvsupport@pearson.com**.

Source: user-provided *MCT Renewal Assessment Information Guide*, pages 1–5, and portal announcement, November 2026. Check [official assessment guide](https://aka.ms/MCT_Renewal_Assessment) before committing.

# Teach-back preparation (unofficial)

According to the provided renewal assessment guide, the live teach-back is **AI-moderated in Yoodli**, requires **≥70%**, and the **best of the first two counted attempts** is used.

**Read the official documents first:** [Teach-back slides](https://aka.ms/Teachback-Slides) · [Rubric](https://aka.ms/MCT-Rubric) · [Yoodli aid](https://aka.ms/Yoodli_Job_Aid).

We have not reproduced those documents. The actual topic, slide requirements, scoring rubric and time limits must come from Microsoft's instructions. The examples below are **only optional rehearsal scenarios** and may not be permitted or relevant to the actual test.

## Practice outline

- **Opening:** Who is the audience? Why is the topic relevant?
- **Objective:** State one observable outcome.
- **Concept:** Explain an idea plainly; define acronyms.
- **Demonstration:** Show a small stable example while narrating the reasoning.
- **Check:** Ask a short question to confirm understanding.
- **Wrap-up:** Repeat the key result and one next step.

**Self-review prompts, not official rubric criteria:** Is the objective clear? Can a beginner follow the explanation? Are visuals readable? Is the demo stable? Do you check understanding and summarize?

## Technical preparation

Use a quiet room, stable internet, camera, microphone, speakers and working screen share. Close notifications. Practice with your slides and live demo before attempting. Pause only *between* modules. Start a scored activity only when ready to finish.

Try the [Azure VM sample](azure-vm-demo.md) or [Docker sample](docker-demo.md) for optional practice. The example durations are **not official assessment timings**.
